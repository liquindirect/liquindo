Edward Fullwood is an English politician known for his right-wing views. He is also the leader of the Liquindo Nationalist Front, a political faction in Liquindo.

Liquindo

Edward Fullwood became affiliated with Liquindo after a visit to Diego Liquin's house. They became best friends as they compromised ideologically.

The LNF was founded on the 16th December 2023 on the promise of being a political party; however, it was later absorbed as a political faction into the Liquin's Plan administration.

The LNF remains Liquindo's biggest faction with 220 members. Even though Fullwood holds a position of power, he is pessimistic about the eventual reality of the nation.

Ideology

Fullwood is a self-described monarcho-socialist and Eurosceptic. He is critical of the European Union's immigration policies and has said that he preferred the EU when it functioned principally as a trade bloc, rather than as a political union which he believes threatens British sovereignty.

Fullwood is also a supporter of the British Empire and has expressed pride in his Anglo-Saxon heritage.

Fullwood has expressed opposition to interracial relationships and concern about demographic change. Some of these views overlap with ideas associated with the "Great Replacement", a far-right conspiracy theory concerning demographic change.

He has also expressed anti-Islam views. He has connected these views to an account involving his aunt, who he says was groomed by a Muslim former boyfriend and forced to wear a hijab.
