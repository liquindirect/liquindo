Encyclopedia of Liquindo

  • Liquindo

    Liquindo, officially the Federal Republic of Liquindo, is envisioned as a crowdfunded island nation; a refuge from global instability, an experimental society blending modern innovation, traditional values, and self-sufficiency.

  • Liquin's Plan

    Liquin's Plan (of Action), abbreviated L'PA, is the founding and current ruling administration of Liquindo.

  • Liquindo Nationalist Front

    The Liquindo Nationalist Front (LNF) is a political faction of Liquindo focused on nationalism, national identity, social discipline, hierarchy, and demographic policy.

  • Liquindian Integralist Front

    The Liquindian Integralist Front is a political faction in Liquindo associated with Catholic integralism, distributism, and the proposed union of Church and state.

  • Nuclear Proliferation Faction

    The Nuclear Proliferation Faction (NPF) is a political faction in Liquindo associated with nuclear deterrence and containing both a standard nationalist and fringe accelerationist current.
