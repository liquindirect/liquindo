Encyclopedia of Liquindo

  • Liquindo

    Liquindo, officially the Federal Republic of Liquindo, is envisioned as a crowdfunded island nation; a refuge from global instability, an experimental society blending modern innovation, traditional values, and self-sufficiency.

  • Liquin's Plan

    Liquin's Plan (of Action) — abbreviated L'PA — is the founding and current ruling administration of Liquindo. It has been described as meritocratic, authoritarian, and autocratic in its structure and approach to governance.

  • Liquindo Nationalist Front

    The Liquindo Nationalist Front (LNF) is a political faction of Liquindo whose ideology emphasizes nationalism, national identity, demographic policy, social discipline, hierarchy, and the preservation of Liquindian culture and heritage.

  • Liquindian Integralist Front

    The Liquindian Integralist Front, commonly referred to as the Liquindian Integralists, is a political faction in Liquindo founded with the objective of increasing Catholic influence and uniting Church and state.
