Liquin's Plan (of Action) — abbreviated L'PA — is the founding and current ruling administration of Liquindo. It has been described as meritocratic, authoritarian, and autocratic in its structure and approach to governance. The party was founded by Diego Liquin in 2024, a few years after the initial development of Liquindo began in 2021. It regards itself not as a political party but a synonym to the state, due to Liquindo's illiberal ideology.

History

Liquin's Plan was formally established in late 2024, partly in response to the earlier formation of the Liquindo Nationalist Front (LNF) which was formed on 16 December 2023. The LNF was rather a movement, rather than a political entity of nationalist populism, while Liquin's Plan asserted itself as the formal authority Liquindo would be developed and ruled by. Pressured by Diego Liquin, the LNF was forced to assimilate as a faction of the party as opposed to it being a rival entity. Alongside this, the LNF had ties to the Vaughtonologists who the L'PA sought to destroy to preserve order.

Vexillology

The state flag includes the navy and mustard horizontal yin and yang shown on the national flag, except in this version, the blots are replaced with new yin and yangs.

Ethnic Objectives

True Liquindians

A stated long-term objective of Liquin's Plan is the creation of a native-born population, referred to as True Liquindians. This goal involves increasing birth rates to transition the nation into Stage 2 of the Demographic Transition Model.

One policy proposal, attributed to Chief Demographicist Diego Liquin, outlines a controlled immigration and reproduction system. In this model, immigrant women would be granted special visas for the purpose of forming family units with native Liquindian men. Each pairing would be limited to a set number of children (typically three), ensuring manageable population growth while fostering cultural continuity.

Children born through these partnerships would be recognized as first-generation Liquindian citizens and raised through national education and ideological programs. Political rights for immigrant participants would be delayed or conditional upon integration metrics such as childbearing or civic contribution.

Proponents of this model argue that it allows for rapid population growth while maintaining a coherent cultural and ideological foundation. Critics, however, have raised concerns regarding ethical considerations and the implications of such demographic engineering.

Eugenics

Prospective parents may be evaluated for genetic compatibility in order to minimize the incidence of hereditary conditions or developmental challenges. The stated rationale is to reduce the burden on public health systems and improve long-term wellbeing. The inclusion of this policy has led to controversy, with external observers questioning its ethical validity.

Cultural Objectives

Return to Cellular Communication

The party strongly advocates abandoning internet-based communication services and phasing out the use of smartphones. It promotes a return to older, simpler technology: people would stop using smartphones outside the home and instead rely on basic flip phones for voice calls only. To replace modern mobile internet use, the party proposes installing personal computers in every home, making them the primary way citizens access digital services.

Return to a Hetero-Normative Society and the War on Drugs

The party calls for a return to what it describes as a "hetero-normative social framework." As part of this stance, it argues that the widespread availability of pornography has negative effects on relationships, mental health, and social values, and therefore supports banning its production and distribution. The party also opposes the legal recognition of same-sex marriage as well as the fostering of children by homosexuals and seeks to restrict gender-transition practices. Its platform frames these positions as being based on its interpretation of scientific and social positivity, although these claims are widely debated and contested.

The party also supports a full ban on all recreational drugs, arguing that eliminating non-medical drug use would improve public discipline, reduce addiction-related harms, and strengthen the nation's overall social and economic stability. This position is presented as part of its broader emphasis on traditional moral values and its belief that personal behaviour should contribute to national well-being.

Migrant Assessment Operation

This policy involves screening incoming migrants based on factors such as criminal background, nationality, financial status, and perceived social compatibility. Individuals assessed as high-risk may face deportation or, in extreme cases, state-enforced removal. The policy has been described by advocates as a security measure, though it has received criticism for potential human rights concerns.

Economic Objectives

The Work-Based Housing Allocation System

The WBHAS replaces private property ownership with a state-controlled, merit-based housing system. Citizens earn access to housing based on a Housing Value Score (HVS), which is calculated using labor output, community service, ideological compliance, and other civic indicators.

High scores grant access to more desirable accommodations, such as oceanfront homes or units near public infrastructure. Low scores may result in reassignment to less favourable housing, or in severe cases, relocation to corrective labour or rehabilitation zones. There is no rent or ownership; instead, housing is viewed as a privilege earned through service to the nation.

Supporters of the WBHAS describe it as an equitable model that prioritizes collective good over individual entitlement. Critics have labelled the system coercive and potentially exclusionary, particularly for dissenters or those unable to work due to health or age.

Factions

There are numerous factions within Liquin's Plan, which work together instead of competing, thus they represent interpretations of the state. Only selected factions are approved, they are representations of the populace, and hold little power considering the absolute power of the director. Factions are ideological neighbours but can still clash.
