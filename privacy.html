Privacy notice

This notice explains the visitor access logging used on the Liquindo website.

What is recorded

For basic site security and administration, the site records the visitor's IP address and the time of the visit. If you are signed in to a Liquindo account, the log also associates that visit with your account. Visitors who are not signed in are labelled anonymous.

The visitor log does not record which pages you viewed, URLs, or a page-by-page browsing history.

Retention and access

Visitor access log entries are automatically deleted once they are more than seven days old. The log is stored in the Liquindo Supabase database and is available only through a server-side administrator check to accounts whose Liquindo profile role is admin.

Questions

For questions about this logging, contact liquindohq@yahoo.com.
