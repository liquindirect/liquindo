Liquindo Nationalist Front

Political faction of Liquindo

Liquindo Nationalist Front
Abbreviation LNF
Country Liquindo
Type Political faction
Youth wing Liquindo Youth

The Liquindo Nationalist Front (LNF) is a political faction associated with Liquindo. Its political program emphasizes nationalism, demographic continuity, social discipline, hierarchy, national service, and the preservation of a defined Liquindian identity.

Some of the faction's proposed policies, particularly those concerning race, immigration, political education, disability, and reproduction, are highly discriminatory and coercive. The descriptions below document positions attributed to the LNF rather than presenting them as established policy or endorsing them.

Immigration and Demography

The LNF advocates selective immigration intended to influence Liquindo's long-term demographic composition. It particularly favors immigration from European countries including Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Germany, and Austria. The faction describes this objective as maintaining a form of "European continuity."

According to the LNF's doctrine, these populations are regarded as culturally compatible with, and racially similar to, the identity it envisions for Liquindo. The faction connects this policy to its broader emphasis on tradition, heritage, ancestry, and national cohesion.

The policy explicitly seeks to produce a whiter demographic profile for Liquindo. The LNF portrays this not as exclusion but as defining the intended character of the nation. It argues that racial cohesion contributes to national unity and that demographic policy should therefore be deliberately controlled.

Under this position, immigration is treated primarily as an instrument of demographic and cultural planning rather than principally as an economic or humanitarian matter.

The LNF defends this approach by appealing to national sovereignty and rejects multiculturalism as a central objective for Liquindo. Such racial selection would, however, constitute discriminatory treatment on the basis of race or ethnicity and would be highly controversial outside the faction's own ideological framework.

Liquindo Youth

Liquindo Youth is the proposed national youth organization associated with the Liquindo Nationalist Front. It is conceived as both a cultural organization and an ideological formation program.

Its proposed activities include civic education, national history, physical training, outdoor activities, teamwork, cultural preservation, and education in the political principles of the LNF. Uniforms, ceremonies, and organized public events form part of the organization's proposed culture.

The organization is based on the LNF's belief that the continuation of the nation depends partly upon younger generations adopting a common sense of identity, civic responsibility, order, and national purpose. Its stated objective is therefore to establish strong social bonds between young Liquindians regardless of their town or family background.

Liquindo Youth also emphasizes hierarchy, heritage, discipline, and preparation for future civic service. This could include eventual service in agriculture, public works, community institutions, or national defense.

Within the LNF's proposed system, participation would function as a significant part of the transition from childhood into citizenship. Members who distinguish themselves could be selected for additional training or future positions within Liquindo's institutions.

Because the organization explicitly combines youth activities with political instruction and the transmission of LNF ideology, critics could characterize the model as political indoctrination rather than ordinary civic education. Supporters of the proposal instead describe it as a means of cultivating discipline, national identity, and continuity between generations.

Child Policy

The LNF has proposed extensive state involvement in reproduction and population planning. Under the proposal, couples would be required to report planned pregnancies to the government, with the stated aims of controlling population growth and regulating hereditary health.

More extreme elements of the proposal call for the state to prevent some disabled people from having children and to compel the termination of certain pregnancies where disabilities or genetic conditions are identified.

These proposals amount to coercive reproductive control and contain elements historically associated with eugenics. They would deny reproductive autonomy and discriminate against disabled people. The LNF's stated justification is the prevention of suffering and the management of the nation's future population, although that rationale does not remove the coercive or discriminatory character of the proposed measures.
