The Liquindo Nationalist Front (LNF) is a political faction associated with Liquindo.

Its political program emphasizes nationalism, demographic continuity, social discipline, hierarchy, national service, and the preservation of a defined Liquindian identity.

The faction is led by Edward Fullwood.

Some positions attributed to the LNF involve discriminatory or coercive proposals concerning race, immigration, disability, and reproduction. This article describes those positions rather than endorsing them.

History

Edward Fullwood became affiliated with Liquindo after developing a close political and personal association with Diego Liquin.

The Liquindo Nationalist Front was founded on 16 December 2023 with the intention of operating as a political party.

It was later incorporated into Liquin's Plan and reorganized as an internal political faction rather than a separate rival organization.

The LNF is described within Liquindo as its largest political faction.

Leadership

Edward Fullwood

Edward Fullwood is the leader of the Liquindo Nationalist Front and has played a significant role in the faction's development.

He has described himself as a monarcho-socialist and Eurosceptic and has criticized aspects of European Union integration and immigration policy.

Fullwood has also expressed support for the historical British Empire and has emphasized Anglo-Saxon heritage as part of his political identity.

Some views attributed to Fullwood concerning interracial relationships and demographic change resemble themes associated with the so-called “Great Replacement”, a far-right conspiracy theory about demographic change.

He has also expressed anti-Islam views, which he has connected to personal stories involving members of his family.

Immigration and Demography

The LNF advocates a selective immigration system intended to shape Liquindo's long-term demographic composition.

The faction has expressed preferences for immigration from certain European countries and describes this objective as preserving “European continuity”.

It connects demographic policy to ideas of ancestry, national cohesion, cultural continuity, and heritage.

Some LNF proposals have explicitly sought to influence Liquindo's racial composition. Selection of migrants on racial or ethnic grounds would constitute discriminatory treatment and is one of the most controversial parts of the faction's program.

Within the LNF's political framework, immigration is therefore treated partly as an instrument of demographic and cultural planning rather than solely as an economic or humanitarian issue.

Liquindo Youth

Liquindo Youth is a proposed youth organization associated with the Liquindo Nationalist Front.

Proposed activities include civic education, national history, outdoor activities, teamwork, cultural preservation, and physical recreation.

The organization is also intended to transmit the LNF's political ideas to younger members. Because of this combination of youth activity and partisan political instruction, critics could characterize the proposal as ideological indoctrination rather than neutral civic education.

Supporters instead describe it as a way to promote civic responsibility, shared identity, discipline, and continuity between generations.

Child and Reproductive Policy

The LNF has proposed substantial state involvement in reproduction and population planning.

Some proposals would require planned pregnancies to be reported to state authorities as part of a broader demographic-management system.

More extreme proposals attributed to the faction would restrict reproductive autonomy for some disabled people or in cases involving particular hereditary conditions.

Such policies are associated with coercive eugenics and would raise serious ethical, disability-rights, medical, and human-rights concerns.
