Submit an article revision

You can open this editor from an article’s “Edit this article” link to edit that article directly. Make changes in the visual editor or switch to wiki markup. Your changes are saved as a revision; non-admin edits require administrator approval, while administrator edits publish immediately.

New articles are submitted as drafts and need administrator approval before publication.

Visual mode
LiquinWiki markup guide

Wiki markup mode: choose “Edit wiki markup” for simpler article text. The preview updates as you type. LiquinWiki now supports headings, formatting, internal/external links, nested lists, categories, citations, infoboxes, simple tables, redirects, “See also” boxes and disambiguation notices. It is a practical subset of MediaWiki, not a complete MediaWiki installation.

Citations: place <ref>Source details or [https://example.com source link]</ref> after a sentence. Reuse a named citation with <ref name="source" />. A References section is generated automatically.
