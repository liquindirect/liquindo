Liquindo, officially the Federal Republic of Liquindo, is envisioned as a crowdfunded island nation and experimental society emphasizing self-sufficiency, traditional values, and modern development.

Etymology

The name Liquindo is a play on the Portuguese words lindo and líquido, meaning “beautiful” and “liquid” respectively. The latter refers to water.

History

Liquindo's initial conception dates to 2021, when Diego Liquin was still in secondary school. The idea was inspired by the film Rose Island .

Since 2021, the project has undergone several revivals, most notably involving Finn Vaughton in 2023 and Edward Fullwood in 2025.

The first physical Liquindo flag was produced on 3 July 2025. Other merchandise, including badges and stickers, was later produced.

Development of the Liquindian language began on 16 November 2025 and has since produced more than 200 words.

Vexillology

Liquindo has used several flag designs since its establishment. The current design consists of yellow, navy, and white, with a rotated yin-yang emblem and a star positioned within the central design.

Within Liquindian symbolism, the star represents authority, navy represents integrity, mustard yellow represents the people, and white represents faith.

Government

The following describes proposed institutions. Liquindo has not acquired sovereign territory and these structures are theoretical.

Liquindo is envisioned as a highly centralized political system headed by a director, with advisers and affiliated political factions participating within the wider administration.

Early proposed structure

An earlier proposal described an elective autocratic structure in which the head of government would appoint a council of political advisers. Following the end of a director's tenure, that council would participate in choosing a successor.

Permanent structure

Later proposals describe Liquindo as being administered through Liquin's Plan, with the director serving as head of state. Political organizations would operate as factions within the administrative system rather than as conventional competing political parties.

Defence

Liquindo's theoretical defence doctrine emphasizes national resilience, civil defence, emergency preparedness, territorial protection, and deterrence rather than foreign expansion.

The concept also assigns defence institutions civilian roles during disasters and infrastructure emergencies, including logistics, reconstruction, emergency response, and continuity of essential services.

Proposed military culture emphasizes discipline, public service, institutional responsibility, and defensive readiness.

Economy

Liquindo's proposed economy is based on the principle that economic activity should serve national self-sufficiency, families, social stability, and long-term development.

Strategic industries such as infrastructure, banking, energy, and heavy industry are envisioned as being heavily directed or owned by the state.

Agriculture is envisioned as more decentralized, with family-owned farms forming an important part of rural economic life.

The system places less emphasis on unrestricted market competition and speculative finance, instead proposing substantial state coordination of production and strategic resources.

Domestic production and self-sufficiency are major goals, although limited trade and private economic activity may continue where compatible with wider national objectives.

Overall, the proposed system combines centralized strategic planning with smaller-scale private and family economic activity.

Political Factions

Liquindo's proposed political system replaces conventional competing political parties with internal factions operating within the wider administrative structure.

Factions may represent different political tendencies while remaining subject to the institutional rules of the administration.

Liquin's Plan

Liquin's Plan (of Action), abbreviated L'PA, is described as the founding and current governing administration of Liquindo.

It describes itself as an administrative structure rather than a conventional political party and is associated with a centralized, meritocratic, authoritarian, and autocratic model of government.

History

Liquin's Plan was formally established in late 2024 following the earlier formation of the Liquindo Nationalist Front (LNF) on 16 December 2023.

The LNF initially operated as a nationalist political movement, while Liquin's Plan presented itself as the formal administrative structure through which Liquindo would be developed and governed.

The LNF was later incorporated into Liquin's Plan as an internal faction rather than continuing as a separate rival organization.

Vexillology

The administration's flag incorporates the navy and mustard yin-yang symbolism also used in Liquindo's national imagery.

In this variant, the smaller contrasting circles of the yin-yang are replaced by additional yin-yang designs.

Demographic Objectives

True Liquindians

One long-term objective associated with Liquin's Plan is the development of a stable native-born population described within the project as “True Liquindians”.

Proposals connected with this objective have included policies intended to increase the birth rate and encourage long-term cultural integration.

Earlier proposals also described highly controlled immigration, citizenship, and family-policy systems. Such ideas have raised ethical concerns because state-directed demographic policies can interfere with individual rights and personal autonomy.

Genetic-health proposals

Some earlier policy documents proposed genetic-health screening for prospective parents with the stated aim of reducing hereditary disease.

These proposals have been controversial because coercive or discriminatory reproductive policies raise serious medical, ethical, and human-rights concerns.

Cultural Objectives

Return to Cellular Communication

Liquin's Plan has proposed reducing dependence on smartphones and internet-based communication services outside the home.

Under this concept, simpler mobile phones would primarily be used for calls, while personal computers would remain the main way households access digital services.

Social Policy

Liquin's Plan supports a socially conservative framework based on its interpretation of traditional family and moral values.

Its proposals have included restrictions on pornography, recreational drugs, same-sex marriage, adoption policy, and some gender-transition practices.

These positions are politically and socially contested and have been criticized on grounds including individual liberty, discrimination, and equal treatment.

Migrant Assessment Operation

A proposed migrant-assessment system would evaluate applicants using factors such as criminal history, financial circumstances, and integration requirements.

Critics of such systems argue that migration policy requires clear legal safeguards to avoid discriminatory treatment and violations of due-process rights.

Economic Objectives

Work-Based Housing Allocation System

The Work-Based Housing Allocation System (WBHAS) is a proposed state-managed housing model in which housing would be allocated according to a Housing Value Score (HVS).

The score has been described as taking account of factors such as work, community contribution, and other civic measures.

Higher scores would provide access to more desirable housing, while lower scores could result in reassignment to other accommodation.

Supporters describe the model as a way of allocating scarce housing according to contribution and public need. Critics argue that a system linking housing to political or behavioural compliance could become coercive or discriminatory.

Factions

Liquin's Plan contains several internal political factions. These organizations are presented as different interpretations of the wider state project rather than as independent competing political parties.

Factions may disagree on particular policies but remain subordinate to the wider administrative structure.

The Liquindo Nationalist Front (LNF) is a political faction associated with Liquindo.

Its political program emphasizes nationalism, demographic continuity, social discipline, hierarchy, national service, and the preservation of a defined Liquindian identity.

The faction is led by Edward Fullwood.

Some positions attributed to the LNF involve discriminatory or coercive proposals concerning race, immigration, disability, and reproduction. This article describes those positions rather than endorsing them.

History

Edward Fullwood became affiliated with Liquindo after developing a close political and personal association with Diego Liquin.

The Liquindo Nationalist Front was founded on 16 December 2023 with the intention of operating as a political party.

It was later incorporated into Liquin's Plan and reorganized as an internal political faction rather than a separate rival organization.

The LNF is described within Liquindo as its largest political faction.

Leadership

Edward Fullwood

Edward Fullwood is the leader of the Liquindo Nationalist Front and has played a significant role in the faction's development.

He has described himself as a monarcho-socialist and Eurosceptic and has criticized aspects of European Union integration and immigration policy.

Fullwood has also expressed support for the historical British Empire and has emphasized Anglo-Saxon heritage as part of his political identity.

Some views attributed to Fullwood concerning interracial relationships and demographic change resemble themes associated with the so-called “Great Replacement”, a far-right conspiracy theory about demographic change.

He has also expressed anti-Islam views, which he has connected to personal stories involving members of his family.

Immigration and Demography

The LNF advocates a selective immigration system intended to shape Liquindo's long-term demographic composition.

The faction has expressed preferences for immigration from certain European countries and describes this objective as preserving “European continuity”.

It connects demographic policy to ideas of ancestry, national cohesion, cultural continuity, and heritage.

Some LNF proposals have explicitly sought to influence Liquindo's racial composition. Selection of migrants on racial or ethnic grounds would constitute discriminatory treatment and is one of the most controversial parts of the faction's program.

Within the LNF's political framework, immigration is therefore treated partly as an instrument of demographic and cultural planning rather than solely as an economic or humanitarian issue.

Liquindo Youth

Liquindo Youth is a proposed youth organization associated with the Liquindo Nationalist Front.

Proposed activities include civic education, national history, outdoor activities, teamwork, cultural preservation, and physical recreation.

The organization is also intended to transmit the LNF's political ideas to younger members. Because of this combination of youth activity and partisan political instruction, critics could characterize the proposal as ideological indoctrination rather than neutral civic education.

Supporters instead describe it as a way to promote civic responsibility, shared identity, discipline, and continuity between generations.

Child and Reproductive Policy

The LNF has proposed substantial state involvement in reproduction and population planning.

Some proposals would require planned pregnancies to be reported to state authorities as part of a broader demographic-management system.

More extreme proposals attributed to the faction would restrict reproductive autonomy for some disabled people or in cases involving particular hereditary conditions.

Such policies are associated with coercive eugenics and would raise serious ethical, disability-rights, medical, and human-rights concerns.

Liquindian Integralist Front

The Liquindian Integralist Front, commonly referred to as the Liquindian Integralists, is a political faction associated with Liquindo.

The faction seeks to increase Catholic influence over Liquindo's national identity and political institutions and supports a closer relationship between religious authority and the state.

History

The Liquindian Integralist Front was founded in late December 2025, around the same period as the National Development Faction.

Its formation followed increased Catholic support within parts of the Liquindo Nationalist Front.

The Integralist Front emerged as a separate faction because its supporters favored a substantially stronger role for Catholicism in public and political life than the LNF's more secular leadership.

Ideology

The faction is primarily influenced by Catholic integralism, a political tradition that rejects a strict separation between religious and political life and argues that government should recognize religious moral authority.

Within the Liquindian Integralist Front, this position is combined with social conservatism, Catholic social teaching, and an emphasis on family and community institutions.

Currents

Two broad currents are associated with the faction.

Integralist Current

The mainstream integralist current supports a close relationship between the Catholic Church and the state while retaining distinct religious and civil institutions.

It argues that legislation and public institutions should be influenced by Catholic moral and social principles.

Theocratic Current

A smaller theocratic current favors a substantially more direct role for religious authority in government.

This current differs from mainstream integralism in the degree to which it would subordinate civil political authority to religious institutions.

Policies

Family

The Integralists regard the family as a fundamental social institution and connect family life closely with religious belief.

The faction supports a traditional Catholic conception of marriage and opposes same-sex marriage and same-sex adoption.

Because these proposals would use state policy to privilege one religious understanding of marriage and family life, they would raise questions concerning religious freedom and equal treatment for citizens who do not share the faction's beliefs.

Economics

The faction supports distributism, an economic philosophy influenced by Catholic social teaching.

Distributism seeks to spread ownership of productive property widely rather than concentrating it in either large corporations or the state.

It generally favors small businesses, family-owned enterprises, cooperatives, local institutions, and forms of worker or professional association.

The philosophy is particularly associated with writers such as G. K. Chesterton and Hilaire Belloc and emphasizes ideas including subsidiarity, local decision-making, private property, and community stability.

Morality

The faction holds that public morality should be strongly influenced by Catholic religious teaching.

It has supported legal restrictions on pornography and recreational drug use, describing them as harmful to individuals, families, and wider social life.

Critics of such policies argue that religiously based restrictions on private conduct can conflict with individual liberty, pluralism, and the rights of citizens who follow different moral or religious traditions.

Economic Thought

The Integralist Front's economic philosophy differs both from unrestricted laissez-faire capitalism and from centralized state socialism.

Its distributist approach favors widespread private ownership and local economic institutions while accepting government action intended to prevent excessive concentrations of economic power.

The faction also emphasizes the Catholic principle of subsidiarity, under which social and economic decisions should generally be made at the lowest practical level rather than automatically transferred to a central authority.

Relationship with Liquin's Plan

The Liquindian Integralist Front operates as a political faction within Liquin's Plan.

Within the factional system, its principal focus is religion, Church-state relations, social conservatism, and distributist economic policy.

The Nuclear Proliferation Faction, commonly abbreviated as the NPF, is a political faction associated with Liquindo.

Its political doctrine places particular emphasis on nuclear deterrence, national sovereignty, and the role of nuclear weapons in international relations.

The faction has expressed opposition to the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons and argues that nuclear capability can increase the strategic influence of smaller states.

This article describes positions attributed to the NPF. Nuclear proliferation and threats involving weapons of mass destruction carry severe humanitarian, environmental, and international security risks.

History

The NPF was founded in late December 2025 alongside the National Development Faction.

Its development was partly influenced by historical nationalist political movements that supported independent national nuclear programs.

One cited influence was Brazil's former Party of the Reconstruction of the National Order (PRONA), whose political program included support for a Brazilian nuclear-weapons capability.

Political Doctrine

The NPF views nuclear strategy primarily through the concepts of deterrence, sovereignty, strategic independence, and international power.

Within this framework, supporters argue that possession of a nuclear deterrent can reduce the vulnerability of a small state to coercion by significantly stronger powers.

Critics of this position argue that wider nuclear proliferation increases the probability of miscalculation, escalation, proliferation to additional actors, and catastrophic humanitarian consequences.

Currents

Two broad tendencies have been associated with the NPF: a standard nationalist current and a smaller accelerationist current.

Standard Current

The standard current treats nuclear weapons primarily as an instrument of strategic deterrence.

Its supporters argue that a smaller state may seek a powerful deterrent when conventional military forces would be insufficient against a substantially larger adversary.

This current generally presents deterrence as defensive rather than as a justification for initiating conflict.

Accelerationist Current

A fringe accelerationist tendency has also been associated with the faction.

It has been described as believing that the existing international order must collapse or undergo extreme disruption before a new political order can emerge.

Unlike the standard current, this tendency has historically treated escalating instability and political violence as desirable.

Such ideas are highly controversial because deliberately promoting large-scale conflict or political violence would endanger civilians and undermine international security.

Nuclear Proliferation

Nuclear proliferation refers to the spread of nuclear weapons, weapons-related technology, or weapons programs to additional states or actors.

International efforts to limit proliferation have developed partly from concern that an increasing number of nuclear-armed states raises the risks of nuclear conflict, accidents, miscalculation, and arms races.

The NPF disputes some of these arguments and instead emphasizes the deterrent effect associated with nuclear arsenals.

Relationship with Liquin's Plan

The NPF operates as an internal political faction within Liquin's Plan rather than as an independent political party.

Its primary role within the factional system is to represent a political tendency focused on nuclear policy, national security, strategic independence, and deterrence.
