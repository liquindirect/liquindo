Foundational constitutional document

Supreme Liquindian Covenant

The Supreme Liquindian Covenant establishes the constitutional foundations, institutions, offices and governing principles of Liquindo. It is intended to serve as the supreme law of the state.

Working constitutional text. Articles 1–22 contain wording approved or recovered during the constitutional drafting process. Other outstanding provisions remain under review; proposed or reconstructed wording is not treated as approved. This page is being revised article by article; incomplete sections are not presented as final law.

Chapter I — Fundamental Principles

Article 1 — Supremacy of the Covenant

  1. The Supreme Liquindian Covenant shall be the supreme law of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. All state institutions, public authorities and individuals shall be bound by the Covenant.
  3. No office, individual or institution, including the Liquin, shall exercise authority above the Covenant.
  4. All laws, decrees, regulations and official acts shall conform to the Covenant.
  5. Any provision or act contrary to the Covenant shall have no constitutional authority.

Article 2 — National Sovereignty

  1. Liquindo shall be a fully sovereign and independent republic.
  2. Sovereignty shall reside in the Liquindian nation and shall be exercised through the institutions established by this Covenant.
  3. No foreign state, organisation or external authority shall exercise governing authority over Liquindo.
  4. Liquindo may participate in international cooperation, provided that such cooperation does not compromise its sovereignty, independence or constitutional order.
  5. The state shall safeguard the territorial integrity and right of self-determination of Liquindo.

Article 3 — National Rupturalism

  1. National Rupturalism (NatRupt) shall be the official state ideology of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. National Rupturalism shall constitute the foundational political and philosophical doctrine of the Liquindian state.
  3. The institutions and policies of the Republic shall be guided by the principles of National Rupturalism.
  4. The state shall preserve, develop and apply National Rupturalism in accordance with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant and the national interests of Liquindo.

Article 4 — Territorial Integrity

  1. The territory of the Republic of Liquindo shall constitute an indivisible part of the sovereign state.
  2. The territorial integrity and political independence of Liquindo shall be protected by the state.
  3. No public authority shall surrender, transfer or renounce any part of Liquindian territory except in accordance with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  4. The defence of Liquindo's sovereignty and territorial integrity shall be a fundamental responsibility of the state.

Article 5 — National Identity

  1. The Liquindian nation shall be founded upon a shared civic, cultural and national identity.
  2. Liquindian national identity shall not be defined by race or ancestry.
  3. The state shall promote the development and preservation of Liquindian culture, traditions and national heritage.
  4. All citizens shall form part of the Liquindian nation without distinction based on racial or ancestral origin.
  5. The development of a common national identity shall be encouraged through civic participation, cultural integration and the use of the Liquindian language.

Article 6 — The National Interest

  1. The national interest of Liquindo shall guide the actions and policies of the state.
  2. The state shall prioritise the sovereignty, security, stability and long-term development of the Republic.
  3. The state shall pursue economic and social development for the benefit of the Liquindian nation.
  4. No public authority shall exercise its powers in deliberate disregard of the national interest or the provisions of this Covenant.

Article 7 — Purpose of the State

  1. The state shall safeguard the sovereignty, independence and territorial integrity of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. The state shall pursue the prosperity of the Liquindian nation and the economic independence of Liquindo.
  3. The state shall advance the revolutionary transformation of Liquindian society in accordance with National Rupturalism.
  4. The state shall preserve and develop Liquindian national identity, language, traditions and cultural heritage.
  5. The state shall maintain the continuity, stability and integrity of the constitutional order.

Article 8 — Official Language

  1. Liquindian shall be the sole official language of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. The state shall promote the development, preservation and use of the Liquindian language throughout national life and public institutions.
  3. The use of other languages shall be permitted in private, cultural and personal contexts.

Article 9 — The National Capital

  1. Caput shall be the permanent constitutional capital of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. The principal national institutions of the state shall be situated in Caput.
  3. The relocation of the national capital shall require an amendment to the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 10 — National Symbols

  1. The Republic of Liquindo shall recognise national symbols representing its sovereignty, identity, history and founding principles.
  2. Such symbols shall include the national flag, national emblem, national anthem and any other symbols recognised by the state.
  3. The designs, specifications and official uses of national symbols shall be established by ordinary legislation.

Article 11 — The Form of Government

  1. Liquindo shall be constituted as an Authoritative National Republic.
  2. The Liquin shall serve as the supreme executive authority and embodiment of the Liquindian nation.
  3. The Liquindian Directorate shall serve as the central governing and administrative institution of the state.
  4. The National People's Council shall provide national representation, consultation and institutional scrutiny.
  5. All institutions of government shall exercise their authority in accordance with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 12 — Constitutional Entrenchment

  1. The founding principles of First Liquin, National Rupturalism and the sovereignty and independence of Liquindo shall constitute permanently entrenched principles of the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  2. No amendment, decree, law or institutional decision shall abolish, replace or fundamentally repudiate these principles.
  3. This prohibition shall bind all state institutions, including the Liquin.
  4. All constitutional amendments shall remain subject to these entrenched principles.

Article 13 — Constitutional Amendment Authority

  1. The authority to approve amendments to the Supreme Liquindian Covenant shall belong exclusively to the Liquin.
  2. No amendment shall take effect without the express approval of the Liquin.
  3. All proposed amendments shall be subject to constitutional review by the Liquindian Directorate and National People's Council.
  4. No amendment may violate the permanently entrenched principles established by this Covenant.
  5. The procedures governing the preparation, approval, recording and publication of amendments shall be regulated by this Covenant.

Article 14 — National Motto

  1. The Republic of Liquindo may establish an official national motto.
  2. The national motto shall be determined, amended or replaced through ordinary legislation or formal state designation.
  3. The national motto shall not constitute an entrenched provision of the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 15 — National Development

  1. The state shall pursue the continuous development and advancement of Liquindo.
  2. National development shall encompass economic prosperity, productive capacity, infrastructure, cultural advancement and institutional development.
  3. The state shall pursue these objectives in accordance with National Rupturalism, national sovereignty and the founding principles of First Liquin.

Article 16 — International Relations

  1. Liquindo shall pursue international relations founded upon sovereignty, independence and mutual respect between nations.
  2. The state may participate in international organisations, agreements and cooperative arrangements where these serve the interests of Liquindo.
  3. No international commitment shall supersede the Supreme Liquindian Covenant or compromise the sovereignty and independence of Liquindo.
  4. The state shall support international cooperation and alternative forms of global economic and political cooperation consistent with its national interests.

Article 17 — National Unity

  1. The state shall preserve and strengthen the unity, cohesion and solidarity of the Liquindian nation.
  2. All state institutions shall act in furtherance of national unity and the preservation of the Liquindian national community.
  3. The state shall encourage the participation of citizens in national life and the advancement of common national objectives.

Article 18 — Public Authority

  1. All public authority within Liquindo shall derive from the Liquindian nation and shall be exercised through the institutions established by the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  2. No public authority shall be exercised independently of the constitutional order.
  3. The delegation of authority to any institution or public official shall not diminish the supreme constitutional authority of the Liquin or the supremacy of the Covenant.

Article 19 — Continuity of the State

  1. The Republic of Liquindo shall exist as a permanent and continuous sovereign state.
  2. The death, abdication, incapacity or replacement of any officeholder shall not affect the legal continuity of the state or its institutions.
  3. All state institutions shall maintain the continuity of national administration in accordance with this Covenant.
  4. The succession to the office of Liquin shall be governed by the provisions of this Covenant.

Article 20 — Interpretation of Foundational Principles

  1. The principles established in this Chapter shall guide the interpretation and application of the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  2. The interpretation of these principles may develop alongside the political, economic and cultural development of Liquindo.
  3. No interpretation shall undermine the sovereignty of Liquindo, the founding legacy of First Liquin or the principles of National Rupturalism.

Chapter II — The Liquin

Article 21 — The Office of the Liquin

  1. The Liquin shall be the supreme executive authority of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. The Liquin shall embody and represent the unity, sovereignty and founding principles of the Liquindian nation.
  3. The Liquin shall serve as the supreme guardian of National Rupturalism and the constitutional legacy of First Liquin.
  4. The office of Liquin shall be held for life, subject to voluntary abdication in accordance with this Covenant.
  5. The authority and responsibilities of the Liquin shall be exercised in accordance with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 22 — Executive Authority

  1. The Liquin shall possess supreme executive authority over the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. The Liquin may exercise executive authority directly or delegate its exercise to the Liquindian Directorate, the National Chancellor or other authorised state officials.
  3. All delegated executive authority shall derive from the Liquin and remain subject to the Liquin's direction, supervision and revocation.
  4. No delegation of executive authority shall diminish the supreme constitutional position of the Liquin.

Article 23 — Legislative Authority

  1. Ultimate legislative authority shall reside in the Liquin.
  2. The Liquindian Directorate may prepare and submit legislative proposals to the Liquin.
  3. The Liquin shall possess the authority to enact, amend, reject or repeal legislation.
  4. The Liquin may issue executive orders having the force of law, provided that such orders conform to the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  5. The National People's Council may submit legislative proposals and recommendations but shall not possess independent legislative authority.
  6. All legislation and executive orders shall remain subordinate to the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 24 — Executive Orders

  1. The Liquin shall possess the authority to issue executive orders for the administration and governance of the Republic.
  2. Executive orders may direct the activities of the Liquindian Directorate and other subordinate state institutions.
  3. All executive orders shall be binding upon the institutions and officials to whom they are addressed.
  4. Executive orders shall not contradict or override the Supreme Liquindian Covenant or legislation enacted by the Liquin.
  5. The Liquin may amend or revoke any executive order.

Article 25 — The National Chancellor

  1. The National Chancellor shall serve as the principal administrative official of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. The National Chancellor shall coordinate the administration and implementation of national government policy.
  3. The National Chancellor shall oversee the administrative coordination of the Liquindian Directorate and the national administration, subject to the authority of the Liquin.
  4. The National Chancellor shall be accountable to the Liquin and subject to scrutiny by the National People's Council.
  5. The National Chancellor shall exercise only those powers conferred by the Liquin or established by this Covenant.
  6. The National Chancellor shall not possess authority superior to or independent of the Liquin.

Article 26 — Powers and Responsibilities of the National Chancellor

  1. The National Chancellor shall supervise the execution of government policy and the coordination of national administration.
  2. The National Chancellor shall coordinate the administrative responsibilities assigned to the Directorate and ensure their implementation in accordance with the instructions of the Liquin.
  3. The National Chancellor shall ensure that the administrative activities of the Directorate are carried out efficiently and in accordance with the instructions of the Liquin.
  4. The National Chancellor may issue administrative instructions within the scope of their delegated authority.
  5. The National Chancellor shall report to the Liquin on the functioning and performance of the national administration.

Article 27 — Appointment of the National Chancellor

  1. The National Chancellor shall be appointed by the Liquin.
  2. The appointment shall be made at the discretion of the Liquin.
  3. The Liquin shall possess the authority to dismiss or replace the National Chancellor.
  4. The National Chancellor shall hold office for as long as they retain the confidence of the Liquin.
  5. The appointment and dismissal of the National Chancellor shall be formally recorded by the state.

Article 28 — Vacancy in the Office of National Chancellor

  1. The office of National Chancellor shall become vacant upon death, resignation, dismissal or permanent incapacity of its holder.
  2. The Liquin shall appoint a replacement National Chancellor.
  3. Pending the appointment of a replacement, the Liquin may designate an authorised official to perform the Chancellor's administrative duties.
  4. Any temporary designation shall not confer permanent entitlement to the office of National Chancellor.

Article 29 — Accountability of the National Chancellor

  1. The National Chancellor shall remain accountable to the Liquin for the administration of the Republic.
  2. The National Chancellor shall provide reports and explanations concerning national administration when requested by the Liquin.
  3. The National People's Council shall possess the authority to question and scrutinise the National Chancellor concerning the performance of their duties.
  4. The National Chancellor shall cooperate with legitimate scrutiny by the National People's Council.
  5. The National Chancellor shall not obstruct the constitutional scrutiny of their conduct or administration.

Article 30 — Removal of the National Chancellor

  1. The Liquin shall possess the exclusive authority to remove the National Chancellor from office.
  2. The Liquin may remove the National Chancellor at any time and without requiring the approval of another state institution.
  3. The National People's Council may submit a formal recommendation to the Liquin concerning the removal of the National Chancellor.
  4. Such a recommendation shall be advisory and shall not independently remove the National Chancellor.
  5. Removal from office shall take effect upon the formal decision of the Liquin.

Article 31 — Appointment of Senior State Officials

  1. The Liquin shall possess the authority to appoint and dismiss senior national officials, except where this Covenant establishes a specific appointment procedure.
  2. Such appointments may include heads of national agencies, senior administrators and national representatives.
  3. The Liquin may reorganise, replace or abolish offices falling within this Article, subject to the provisions of this Covenant.
  4. All officials appointed under this Article shall exercise their responsibilities under the authority of the Liquin and in accordance with the Covenant.

Article 32 — Consultation with the National People's Council

  1. The Liquin may consult the National People's Council on matters of national importance at their discretion.
  2. The Liquin shall not be constitutionally required to obtain the Council's approval before exercising executive authority.
  3. The National People's Council shall possess the authority to question and scrutinise the National Chancellor and members of the Liquindian Directorate.
  4. The National People's Council may formally raise concerns regarding the constitutional conduct of the government.
  5. Consultation with the National People's Council shall not diminish the supreme authority of the Liquin under this Covenant.

Article 33 — Constitutional Responsibility of the Liquin

  1. The Liquin shall bear supreme responsibility for upholding and preserving the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  2. The Liquindian Directorate shall bear collective responsibility for administering the state in accordance with the Covenant.
  3. Individual members of the Directorate shall be responsible for ensuring that their respective duties and portfolios conform to the Covenant.
  4. Allegations that the Liquin has violated the Covenant shall be jointly assessed by the Liquindian Directorate and the National People's Council.
  5. Any findings arising from such an assessment shall be formally recorded.

Article 34 — Legal Immunity of the Liquin

  1. The Liquin shall enjoy complete legal immunity while holding office.
  2. The Liquin shall not be subject to ordinary criminal or civil proceedings during their tenure.
  3. Such immunity shall extend to both official acts and private conduct.
  4. Constitutional assessment under Article 33 shall remain distinct from ordinary criminal or civil proceedings.
  5. Nothing in this Article shall exempt the Liquin from the constitutional responsibility to uphold the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 35 — The National People's Council

  1. The National People's Council shall serve as the principal national representative and consultative institution of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. The Council shall provide a formal institution through which citizens may participate in national political affairs.
  3. The Council shall exercise its functions in accordance with the authority and limitations established by this Covenant.
  4. The Council shall not possess independent executive or legislative authority.
  5. The composition, membership and internal organisation of the Council shall be regulated by this Covenant and applicable legislation.

Article 36 — Composition and Membership of the National People's Council

  1. The National People's Council shall be composed of representatives selected in accordance with procedures established by law.
  2. The law shall determine the number of representatives, their terms of service, eligibility requirements and the method by which they are selected.
  3. The composition of the Council shall provide for the representation of the Liquindian nation and the constituent vichies.
  4. All members of the Council shall exercise their responsibilities in accordance with this Covenant and the laws of the Republic.

Article 37 — Functions of the National People's Council

  1. The National People's Council shall deliberate on matters affecting the Republic and the interests of the Liquindian nation.
  2. The Council may adopt resolutions, recommendations and formal opinions for submission to the Liquin and the relevant state institutions.
  3. The Council shall scrutinise the administration of the Republic, including the work of the National Chancellor and the Liquindian Directorate, in accordance with this Covenant.
  4. The Council may request information and explanations from officials subject to its constitutional scrutiny.
  5. The exercise of the Council's functions shall remain subject to the constitutional authority of the Liquin and shall not confer independent executive or legislative power upon the Council.

Article 38 — Sessions of the National People's Council

  1. The National People's Council shall convene in regular sessions in accordance with procedures established by law.
  2. Extraordinary sessions may be convened when required by the national interest or the proper exercise of the Council's constitutional functions.
  3. The Liquin may request that the Council convene to consider matters of national importance.
  4. The timing, duration and notice of sessions shall be regulated by law and the Council's internal rules, subject to this Covenant.

Article 39 — Resolutions and Recommendations of the National People's Council

  1. The National People's Council may adopt resolutions, recommendations and opinions within the scope of its constitutional functions.
  2. Resolutions and recommendations shall express the position of the Council and may be submitted to the Liquin, the Liquindian Directorate or other relevant state institutions.
  3. Such resolutions, recommendations and opinions shall not have the force of legislation or executive orders unless given legal effect through the procedures established by this Covenant.
  4. The Liquin and relevant state institutions may consider and respond to submissions made by the Council.

Article 40 — Internal Organisation of the National People's Council

  1. The National People's Council shall regulate its internal proceedings and administrative arrangements in accordance with this Covenant and applicable law.
  2. The Council may establish committees or other internal bodies to assist it in carrying out its functions.
  3. The procedures for presiding over sessions, conducting deliberations, recording decisions and maintaining official records shall be established by law or the Council's internal rules.
  4. No internal rule or decision of the Council shall exceed the authority conferred upon it by this Covenant.

Chapter III — Liquindian Directorate

Article 41 — Establishment of the Liquindian Directorate

  1. The Liquindian Directorate shall be established as the central governing and administrative institution of the Republic.
  2. The Directorate shall administer national policy and coordinate the ordinary functions of government under the authority of the Liquin.
  3. The Directorate shall exercise only those powers conferred upon it by this Covenant, legislation or the Liquin.
  4. The Directorate shall remain subordinate to the constitutional authority of the Liquin and bound by the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 42 — Composition of the Liquindian Directorate

  1. The Liquindian Directorate shall consist of the National Chancellor, Directors appointed to serve within the Directorate and political advisors appointed by the Liquin.
  2. The organisation and membership of the Directorate shall be determined in accordance with this Covenant and decisions of the Liquin.
  3. Members of the Directorate shall be assigned responsibilities in accordance with their offices and portfolios.
  4. The Directorate shall operate collectively while its individual members remain responsible for their assigned duties.

Article 43 — Responsibilities of the Liquindian Directorate

  1. The Directorate shall implement national policy and administer the affairs of the Republic in accordance with the instructions of the Liquin.
  2. The Directorate shall coordinate the work of the Republic's administrative institutions and bodies placed under its authority.
  3. The Directorate shall advise and report to the Liquin on matters concerning national administration and the implementation of state policy.
  4. The Directorate shall support the continuity, efficiency and lawful operation of the national administration.
  5. The Directorate shall perform its responsibilities in accordance with National Rupturalism and the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 44 — Duties of Directorate Members

  1. Each member of the Liquindian Directorate shall discharge the responsibilities assigned to them by the Liquin or under this Covenant.
  2. Members shall act in coordination with the National Chancellor and other members of the Directorate.
  3. Each member shall keep the Liquin and National Chancellor informed of matters materially affecting the performance of their responsibilities.
  4. No member shall exceed the authority attached to their office or delegated to them by the Liquin.
  5. Members shall uphold the constitutional order and act in accordance with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 45 — Collective Conduct of the Directorate

  1. The Liquindian Directorate shall conduct its responsibilities collectively while respecting the distinct duties assigned to each member.
  2. Members shall cooperate in the consideration and implementation of matters affecting more than one area of national administration.
  3. Differences of administrative opinion may be presented to the National Chancellor or referred to the Liquin for determination.
  4. No internal arrangement or collective decision shall limit the supreme authority of the Liquin.

Article 46 — Direction and Coordination of the Directorate

  1. The Liquin shall retain supreme authority over the direction and operation of the Liquindian Directorate.
  2. The National Chancellor shall coordinate the Directorate's administration and the execution of its assigned responsibilities, subject to the authority of the Liquin.
  3. The Liquin may issue instructions to the Directorate or any of its members and may alter the allocation of administrative responsibilities.
  4. No member or administrative body of the Directorate shall exercise authority independently of the constitutional order or contrary to the instructions of the Liquin.

Article 47 — Appointment of Directorate Members

  1. Members of the Liquindian Directorate shall be appointed by the Liquin.
  2. The Liquin shall determine the offices to be filled and the responsibilities assigned to each appointee.
  3. Appointments shall take effect upon formal designation by the Liquin.
  4. The appointment of the National Chancellor shall remain subject to the specific provisions of Articles 27 and 28.

Article 48 — Eligibility and Qualifications

  1. A person appointed to the Liquindian Directorate shall be a citizen of Liquindo.
  2. Appointees shall possess the competence, integrity and commitment necessary to discharge the responsibilities of their office.
  3. The Liquin may establish further qualifications or conditions for particular offices.
  4. No qualification or condition established under this Article shall override the provisions of this Covenant.

Article 49 — Tenure of Directorate Members

  1. Members of the Liquindian Directorate shall hold office at the confidence and discretion of the Liquin.
  2. The Liquin may determine the duration or conditions of an appointment.
  3. Unless otherwise specified by the Liquin, an appointment shall continue until resignation, dismissal, incapacity or the occurrence of another lawful vacancy.
  4. Continued service shall not create an entitlement to remain in office.

Article 50 — Dismissal and Resignation of Directorate Members

  1. The Liquin shall possess the authority to dismiss any member of the Liquindian Directorate, subject to the specific provisions governing the National Chancellor.
  2. A member may submit a resignation to the Liquin.
  3. A resignation shall take effect when accepted by the Liquin or on such later date as the Liquin determines.
  4. The dismissal or resignation of a member shall be formally recorded.

Article 51 — Vacancies in the Directorate

  1. A vacancy in the membership of the Liquindian Directorate shall arise upon death, resignation, dismissal or permanent incapacity.
  2. The Liquin shall determine whether the vacant office is to be filled, reorganised or discontinued.
  3. The Liquin may appoint an authorised official to perform necessary duties pending a permanent appointment.
  4. A temporary assignment shall not confer permanent appointment to the vacant office.

Article 52 — Meetings of the Directorate

  1. The Liquindian Directorate shall meet when convened by the Liquin or the National Chancellor acting under the Liquin's authority.
  2. The Liquin may preside over any meeting of the Directorate.
  3. In the absence of the Liquin, the National Chancellor shall coordinate proceedings unless the Liquin has designated another presiding official.
  4. The Liquin may determine the frequency, format and procedure of Directorate meetings.

Article 53 — Deliberation and Decisions

  1. The Directorate may deliberate on matters of national administration and the implementation of state policy.
  2. It may formulate advice, recommendations and administrative proposals for submission to the Liquin.
  3. Any decision of the Directorate shall remain within the authority conferred upon it and shall be subject to the direction of the Liquin.
  4. No deliberation, recommendation or decision of the Directorate shall restrict the Liquin's constitutional powers.

Article 54 — Official Records

  1. The Directorate shall maintain accurate records of its formal meetings, decisions, recommendations and official acts.
  2. Records shall be preserved as part of the official archives of the Republic.
  3. The Liquin may determine requirements for the preparation, custody, access, publication and preservation of such records.
  4. Records shall be made available to the competent state authorities when lawfully required.

Article 55 — Reports to the Liquin

  1. The Directorate shall report to the Liquin on the execution of national policy and the condition of the national administration.
  2. The Liquin may require reports from the Directorate collectively or from any individual member.
  3. Reports shall be accurate, timely and sufficient to enable the Liquin to exercise effective constitutional supervision.
  4. The Directorate shall promptly communicate any matter that may materially affect the sovereignty, security, stability or administration of the Republic.

Article 56 — Administrative Coordination

  1. The Directorate shall promote coordination and consistency across the national administration.
  2. Where responsibilities overlap, the National Chancellor shall seek to ensure cooperation and resolve administrative difficulties within the authority delegated by the Liquin.
  3. Matters that cannot be resolved within that delegated authority shall be referred to the Liquin for determination.
  4. Administrative coordination shall not alter the constitutional responsibilities or authority of any institution without lawful direction.

Article 57 — Organisation of the National Administration

  1. The national administration shall be organised in a manner that enables the effective execution of the functions of the Republic.
  2. Its administrative structure shall operate under the constitutional authority of the Liquin and the coordinating responsibility of the National Chancellor.
  3. All administrative offices and bodies shall exercise only the responsibilities and powers lawfully assigned to them.
  4. The organisation of the national administration shall remain consistent with this Covenant.

Article 58 — Authority to Reorganise Administrative Structures

  1. The Liquin shall possess exclusive authority to establish, abolish, merge, divide or reorganise the administrative structures of the Republic, subject to this Covenant.
  2. The Liquin may assign, transfer or withdraw administrative responsibilities and determine the offices through which they shall be exercised.
  3. The National Chancellor and the Directorate shall implement any such direction issued by the Liquin.
  4. No administrative arrangement shall limit or diminish the constitutional authority of the Liquin.

Article 59 — Relations with the National People's Council

  1. The Directorate shall provide information and explanations to the National People's Council in accordance with the Council's constitutional scrutiny powers and applicable law.
  2. The Directorate may submit reports, proposals and advice to the Council where consultation is appropriate or requested by the Liquin.
  3. The Directorate shall consider resolutions and recommendations of the National People's Council and may provide a formal response.
  4. Nothing in this Article shall confer executive or legislative authority upon the Council or diminish the authority of the Liquin.

Article 60 — Continuity and Constitutional Compliance

  1. The Directorate shall maintain the continuity of national administration during vacancies, transitions and other disruptions affecting public administration.
  2. Its members and subordinate administrative bodies shall continue to perform their lawful duties unless directed otherwise by the Liquin.
  3. All acts of the Directorate shall comply with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant and the laws and executive orders of the Republic.
  4. The Directorate shall not suspend, disregard or alter the constitutional order by reason of administrative necessity.

Chapter IV — Succession and Continuity

Article 61 — Continuity of the Office of Liquin

  1. The office of Liquin shall be maintained as a permanent institution of the Republic.
  2. The death, abdication or permanent incapacity of a Liquin shall initiate the succession arrangements established by this Covenant.
  3. No vacancy in the office shall dissolve, suspend or diminish the sovereignty or legal continuity of the Republic.
  4. All state institutions shall continue to operate in accordance with this Covenant during a succession.

Article 62 — Principle of Succession

  1. Succession to the office of Liquin shall be conducted in an orderly manner and in accordance with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  2. The succession process shall safeguard the sovereignty, stability and constitutional continuity of Liquindo.
  3. No person shall acquire the office of Liquin solely by claiming a right of succession outside the procedures established under this Covenant.
  4. The succession process shall be guided by the founding principles of the Republic and National Rupturalism.

Article 63 — The Cadre System

  1. The selection of a successor Liquin shall be conducted through a national cadre system.
  2. The cadre system shall identify and assess individuals considered capable of assuming the responsibilities of the office.
  3. Assessment shall have regard to leadership, competence, integrity, political understanding and commitment to the constitutional order.
  4. The detailed operation of the cadre system shall be regulated in accordance with this Covenant.

Article 64 — Preparation of Successor Candidates

  1. The state shall maintain appropriate arrangements for identifying and preparing potential candidates for future succession.
  2. Preparation may include experience in public service, national administration, leadership and the institutions of the Republic.
  3. Participation in preparatory arrangements shall not create an automatic entitlement to succeed to the office of Liquin.
  4. All candidates shall remain subject to the assessment and selection procedures established under the cadre system.

Article 65 — Qualifications of a Successor

  1. A successor Liquin shall be a citizen of Liquindo.
  2. A candidate shall demonstrate the ability to uphold the Supreme Liquindian Covenant and preserve the sovereignty of the Republic.
  3. A candidate shall possess the judgement, competence and integrity required for the supreme responsibilities of the office.
  4. Further qualifications may be prescribed by the procedures governing the cadre system, provided that they conform to this Covenant.

Article 66 — Assessment of Candidates

  1. Candidates shall be assessed through the cadre system according to consistent and relevant criteria.
  2. The assessment shall consider each candidate's record of service, capacity for leadership and understanding of the constitutional responsibilities of the Liquin.
  3. Assessment shall also consider the candidate's commitment to the founding principles and national interests of Liquindo.
  4. The procedures and records of assessment shall be maintained in accordance with rules established under this Covenant.

Article 67 — Role of the Liquindian Directorate

  1. The Liquindian Directorate shall participate in the succession framework in the manner prescribed by this Covenant and the procedures governing the cadre system.
  2. The Directorate shall support the orderly administration of the succession process and the continuity of national government.
  3. The Directorate may provide assessments, records and recommendations concerning candidates in accordance with the applicable procedures.
  4. The Directorate shall not assume the office of Liquin or acquire the permanent powers of that office by reason of a vacancy.

Article 68 — Determination of the Successor

  1. The successor to the office of Liquin shall be determined through the cadre system and the succession procedure established under this Covenant.
  2. The procedure shall identify the person selected to assume the office and provide for the formal confirmation of that selection.
  3. No recommendation, assessment or preliminary designation shall by itself confer the office unless the required succession procedure has been completed.
  4. The detailed stages and authorities involved in the determination shall be prescribed by a procedure consistent with this Covenant.

Article 69 — Formal Assumption of Office

  1. A successor shall assume the office of Liquin upon completion of the applicable succession procedure and formal confirmation of the succession.
  2. The assumption of office shall be recorded in an official instrument of the Republic.
  3. The successor shall affirm their commitment to uphold the Supreme Liquindian Covenant and preserve the sovereignty and constitutional order of Liquindo.
  4. The date and manner of the formal assumption of office shall be recorded and made public, subject to lawful requirements of state security.

Article 70 — Death of the Liquin

  1. Upon the death of the Liquin, the state shall formally record the vacancy in the office.
  2. The succession procedure shall be initiated without unnecessary delay.
  3. The Liquindian Directorate shall maintain the ordinary administration of the Republic during the succession period.
  4. No member of the Directorate shall become Liquin solely by virtue of seniority, office or temporary administrative responsibility.

Article 71 — Voluntary Abdication

  1. The Liquin may voluntarily abdicate the office in accordance with this Covenant.
  2. An abdication shall be expressed in a formal written declaration signed by the Liquin.
  3. The declaration shall specify the effective date of abdication and shall be entered into the official records of the Republic.
  4. The succession procedure shall commence in accordance with the effective date and the applicable succession rules.

Article 72 — Permanent Incapacity

  1. Where the Liquin is alleged to be permanently incapable of performing the essential duties of the office, the matter shall be addressed under a procedure established in accordance with this Covenant.
  2. Any assessment of incapacity shall be based on reliable evidence and conducted with due regard to the continuity of the state.
  3. A declaration of permanent incapacity shall not be made solely on the basis of political disagreement or temporary inability to perform duties.
  4. Where permanent incapacity is formally established, the succession procedure shall be initiated.

Article 73 — Administration During a Vacancy

  1. During a vacancy in the office of Liquin, the Liquindian Directorate shall maintain the ordinary administration of the Republic under the provisions of this Covenant.
  2. The National Chancellor shall coordinate the continuity of administrative functions.
  3. During this period, the Directorate may take measures necessary for the routine operation of the state and the preservation of public order.
  4. Measures that would permanently alter the constitutional structure or predetermine the succession shall not be taken under the authority of this Article.

Article 74 — Limits on Authority During a Vacancy

  1. No official shall claim or exercise the office of Liquin merely by reason of a vacancy.
  2. The temporary administration of the Republic shall not constitute a transfer of the supreme office or its permanent constitutional powers.
  3. During a vacancy, state institutions shall refrain from actions intended to obstruct or improperly influence the succession procedure.
  4. All temporary administrative measures shall remain subject to this Covenant and shall cease or be reviewed upon the assumption of office by the successor.

Article 75 — Protection of the Succession Process

  1. All institutions and officials participating in succession shall act in accordance with the procedures established under this Covenant.
  2. No person shall unlawfully interfere with the assessment, selection or formal confirmation of a successor.
  3. Records and materials relevant to succession shall be preserved and protected against unauthorised alteration or destruction.
  4. Any dispute concerning the application of the succession procedure shall be addressed through the constitutional procedures of the Republic.

Article 76 — Disclosure and Official Records

  1. The state shall maintain official records of the succession process.
  2. Such records shall include formal decisions, confirmations and instruments necessary to establish the lawful succession.
  3. Information may be withheld where disclosure would present a legitimate risk to national security or the integrity of the succession process.
  4. Any restriction on disclosure shall be consistent with this Covenant and applicable law.

Article 77 — Challenges to Succession

  1. A challenge to the validity of a succession shall identify the alleged breach of this Covenant or the applicable succession procedure.
  2. Challenges shall be submitted and considered through the constitutional procedures established by this Covenant.
  3. The existence of a challenge shall not automatically suspend the ordinary administration of the Republic.
  4. A final determination shall be formally recorded and shall bind the institutions and officials concerned.

Article 78 — First Liquin and the Founding Legacy

  1. Succession to the office of Liquin shall preserve the founding legacy of First Liquin.
  2. Every successor shall uphold the permanently entrenched principles established by this Covenant.
  3. No succession or assumption of office shall authorise the repudiation of the sovereignty, independence or foundational constitutional identity of Liquindo.
  4. The founding legacy shall be interpreted in accordance with the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 79 — Regulation of Succession Procedures

  1. Detailed procedures for the operation of the cadre system, candidate assessment, succession administration and formal assumption of office shall be established by law or regulations made under this Covenant.
  2. Such procedures shall be consistent with the principles and requirements of this Chapter.
  3. No regulation shall create an office or authority that displaces the constitutional position of the Liquin.
  4. Any amendment to the fundamental rules of succession shall be subject to the amendment provisions of this Covenant.

Article 80 — Completion of Succession

  1. Upon the lawful assumption of office by a successor, the succession period shall be deemed complete.
  2. All state institutions shall recognise the successor as the Liquin in accordance with the formal record of succession.
  3. The successor shall assume the powers and responsibilities of the office under this Covenant.
  4. The continuity of the Republic and its institutions shall remain uninterrupted by the completion of succession.

Chapter V — National People's Council

Article 81 — Constitutional Status of the National People's Council

  1. The National People's Council shall be the principal national representative and deliberative institution of the Republic.
  2. The Council shall provide an organised forum for the expression of public concerns and consideration of matters affecting the Liquindian nation.
  3. The Council shall exercise only the representative, consultative and scrutiny functions conferred upon it by this Covenant.
  4. The Council shall remain subject to the constitutional authority of the Liquin.

Article 82 — Representation of the Nation

  1. The National People's Council shall represent the Liquindian nation as a whole.
  2. Its composition shall provide for representation of the constituent vichies of the Republic.
  3. The arrangements for representation shall be established by law in accordance with this Covenant.
  4. No representative shall claim an authority to act independently of the constitutional status and functions of the Council.

Article 83 — Selection of Representatives

  1. Representatives shall be selected in accordance with procedures established by law.
  2. The law shall prescribe the method of selection, the number of representatives and the duration of their service.
  3. Selection procedures shall be consistent with the representative character of the Council and the constitutional order of the Republic.
  4. No method of selection shall confer legislative or executive authority upon the Council.

Article 84 — Eligibility for Membership

  1. Members of the National People's Council shall be citizens of Liquindo.
  2. Further eligibility requirements may be prescribed by law.
  3. Eligibility requirements shall be applied consistently and shall not contradict the rights and principles established by this Covenant.
  4. A member shall disclose any circumstance that may materially affect their ability to discharge their public responsibilities.

Article 85 — Term of Membership

  1. The term of service of Council members shall be determined by law.
  2. The law may prescribe conditions for renewal, replacement or termination of membership.
  3. Membership shall not confer a permanent right to hold office.
  4. The expiry of a member's term shall not invalidate acts lawfully completed by the Council during that member's service.

Article 86 — Vacancies in Membership

  1. A seat in the National People's Council shall become vacant upon death, resignation, removal, disqualification or expiry of the member's term.
  2. Vacancies shall be filled in accordance with the selection procedures established by law.
  3. The Council may continue to perform its functions where vacancies exist, subject to any lawful quorum requirements.
  4. The existence of a vacancy shall not alter the constitutional status of the Council.

Article 87 — Presiding Officer

  1. The National People's Council shall have a presiding officer responsible for coordinating its proceedings.
  2. The method of appointment or selection, term and duties of the presiding officer shall be established by law or the Council's internal rules.
  3. The presiding officer shall maintain order and ensure that proceedings are conducted in accordance with this Covenant.
  4. The presiding officer shall not exercise powers belonging to the Liquin or the executive institutions of the Republic.

Article 88 — Convening of Sessions

  1. The National People's Council shall convene in regular and, where necessary, extraordinary sessions.
  2. The schedule and notice of regular sessions shall be determined by law or the Council's internal rules.
  3. An extraordinary session may be convened by the presiding officer in accordance with the applicable rules or at the request of the Liquin.
  4. The Liquin may require the Council to convene to consider a matter of national importance.

Article 89 — Agenda and Order of Business

  1. The agenda of each session shall be prepared in accordance with the Council's internal rules.
  2. The Liquin may refer matters to the Council for deliberation or advice.
  3. Members may propose matters for consideration in accordance with the procedures established by law or internal rules.
  4. The Council shall not use its agenda or proceedings to assume powers not conferred upon it by this Covenant.

Article 90 — Deliberation

  1. The Council may deliberate on national policy, public administration and matters affecting the interests of the Republic.
  2. Members shall be afforded a reasonable opportunity to express relevant views during proceedings.
  3. Deliberations shall be conducted in an orderly manner and in accordance with the Council's internal rules.
  4. Deliberation shall not itself create binding law or executive direction.

Article 91 — Committees

  1. The National People's Council may establish committees to examine matters within its constitutional functions.
  2. Committees may gather information, consider submissions and prepare reports for the Council.
  3. The composition, remit and procedures of committees shall be determined by the Council's internal rules or applicable law.
  4. No committee shall exercise authority exceeding that of the Council.

Article 92 — Scrutiny of National Administration

  1. The Council shall scrutinise the conduct and performance of the National Chancellor and the Liquindian Directorate within the scope of this Covenant.
  2. Scrutiny may concern the implementation of national policy, the administration of public affairs and compliance with constitutional requirements.
  3. The Council may request relevant explanations and information from officials subject to its scrutiny.
  4. Scrutiny shall not amount to the exercise of executive supervision or direct administrative command.

Article 93 — Requests for Information

  1. The Council may request reports, documents and explanations necessary for the performance of its constitutional functions.
  2. Requests shall be directed to the relevant public authority or official in accordance with applicable procedures.
  3. Information may be withheld where disclosure would create a legitimate risk to national security or another protected public interest.
  4. Any refusal or restriction shall be explained to the Council to the extent permitted by law.

Article 94 — Appearance of Public Officials

  1. The Council may invite or request the appearance of the National Chancellor and members of the Liquindian Directorate for the purpose of scrutiny.
  2. Procedures governing attendance, questioning and the submission of evidence shall be established by law or internal rules.
  3. Officials shall cooperate with lawful requests made within the Council's constitutional authority.
  4. This Article shall not authorise the Council to direct or dismiss an official.

Article 95 — Reports and Findings

  1. The Council may prepare reports setting out its findings on matters considered within its functions.
  2. Reports may include observations, conclusions and recommendations addressed to the Liquin or relevant public institutions.
  3. Reports shall distinguish established information from opinion or recommendation.
  4. The Council shall maintain an official record of reports adopted in accordance with its procedures.

Article 96 — Resolutions and Recommendations

  1. The Council may adopt resolutions and recommendations expressing its considered position on matters within its constitutional functions.
  2. Resolutions and recommendations shall be adopted in accordance with applicable quorum and voting procedures.
  3. They shall be advisory unless the Liquin or a law gives them a specific legal effect consistent with this Covenant.
  4. No resolution or recommendation shall override an executive order or legislation lawfully issued by the Liquin.

Article 97 — Petitions and Public Submissions

  1. The Council may receive petitions, representations and submissions concerning matters of public interest.
  2. Procedures for receiving and considering submissions shall be established by law or internal rules.
  3. The Council may refer submissions to the Liquin or a relevant public authority for consideration.
  4. The receipt of a petition shall not create a right to a particular decision or outcome.

Article 98 — Conduct and Responsibilities of Members

  1. Members shall perform their duties in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.
  2. Members shall act with integrity, respect the proceedings of the Council and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
  3. The Council may establish standards of conduct and procedures for addressing breaches, subject to applicable law.
  4. No member shall use their position to claim executive or legislative authority not granted by this Covenant.

Article 99 — Records and Public Proceedings

  1. The Council shall maintain official records of its sessions, proceedings, resolutions and reports.
  2. Proceedings shall ordinarily be recorded and made accessible to the public in accordance with applicable law.
  3. All or part of a proceeding may be held in private where necessary to protect national security or another legitimate public interest.
  4. Any restriction on public access shall be recorded and justified in accordance with the applicable rules.

Article 100 — Constitutional Limits of the Council

  1. The National People's Council shall not enact legislation, issue executive orders or exercise the governing powers of the Liquin.
  2. The Council shall not appoint or dismiss the Liquin, the National Chancellor or members of the Liquindian Directorate.
  3. The Council's scrutiny and advisory functions shall be exercised within the limits established by this Covenant.
  4. Nothing in this Chapter shall diminish the Council's express role in the constitutional assessment procedure established by Article 33.

Chapter VI — Vichial Government

Article 101 — Territorial Organisation of the Republic

  1. The Republic of Liquindo shall be territorially organised into four constituent vichies: Caput, Edwardia, Vaughtown and Leeborough.
  2. Each vichy shall form an integral part of the sovereign and indivisible Republic.
  3. The vichies shall exercise their functions within the constitutional order and shall not possess sovereignty independent of the Republic.
  4. The boundaries and administrative arrangements of the vichies shall be governed by this Covenant and applicable law.

Article 102 — Status of the Vichies

  1. Each vichy shall possess a recognised territorial and administrative identity within Liquindo.
  2. Vichial institutions shall administer matters assigned to them under this Covenant and the laws of the Republic.
  3. No vichy shall exercise powers reserved to the national institutions of the Republic.
  4. Vichial administration shall remain subordinate to the constitutional authority of the Liquin and the national government.

Article 103 — The Archvichy

  1. Each vichy shall be headed by an Archvichy.
  2. The Archvichy shall serve as the principal vichial authority and shall oversee the executive-administrative government of the vichy.
  3. The Archvichy shall exercise their responsibilities in accordance with this Covenant, national law and the lawful direction of the Liquin.
  4. The office of Archvichy shall not confer sovereignty or authority independent of the Republic.

Article 104 — Appointment and Tenure of Archvichies

  1. The appointment of Archvichies shall be governed by the constitutional authority of the Liquin and procedures established by law or formal state regulation.
  2. The terms and conditions of service of an Archvichy shall be determined in accordance with the applicable appointment arrangements.
  3. An Archvichy shall remain accountable to the Liquin for the discharge of their constitutional responsibilities.
  4. The procedures for resignation, dismissal, replacement and temporary absence shall be established by law or formal state regulation.

Article 105 — Responsibilities of Archvichies

  1. An Archvichy shall oversee the administration of their vichy.
  2. An Archvichy shall coordinate the work of the Vichy Council and ensure that vichial administration conforms to national law.
  3. An Archvichy shall communicate relevant administrative matters to the national government and comply with lawful national directions.
  4. An Archvichy shall promote the welfare and orderly administration of the vichy within the constitutional framework of the Republic.

Article 106 — The Vichy Council

  1. Each vichy shall have a Vichy Council serving as its executive-administrative body.
  2. The Vichy Council shall be composed of the Archvichy and Legates appointed by the Archvichy.
  3. The Vichy Council shall assist the Archvichy in directing and coordinating the administration of the vichy.
  4. The Vichy Council shall remain subject to this Covenant, national law and the authority of the Liquin.

Article 107 — Legates

  1. An Archvichy may appoint Legates to serve within the Vichy Council.
  2. Legates shall carry out the responsibilities assigned to them by the Archvichy or established by applicable law.
  3. Legates shall act under the direction of the Archvichy and within the authority of the Vichy Council.
  4. The appointment, responsibilities, tenure and removal of Legates shall be governed by applicable law or formal regulation.

Article 108 — Functions of the Vichy Council

  1. The Vichy Council shall coordinate the executive and administrative affairs of its vichy.
  2. It shall implement national law and lawful directions within the vichy.
  3. It may advise the Archvichy on matters concerning vichial administration and public welfare.
  4. It shall report to the national government as required by law or lawful instruction.

Article 109 — The Vichial People's Assembly

  1. Each vichy shall have a Vichial People's Assembly as its representative institution.
  2. The Vichial People's Assembly shall provide a forum for representation and deliberation on matters affecting the vichy and its inhabitants.
  3. The Assembly shall be institutionally distinct from the Vichy Council.
  4. The Assembly shall exercise only the functions conferred upon it by this Covenant and applicable law.

Article 110 — Composition of Vichial People's Assemblies

  1. The composition of each Vichial People's Assembly shall be established by law.
  2. Applicable law shall prescribe the number of representatives, their eligibility, method of selection and terms of service.
  3. Arrangements shall provide for representation of the inhabitants of the relevant vichy.
  4. No representative arrangement shall confer sovereign or independent legislative authority upon a Vichial People's Assembly.

Article 111 — Functions of Vichial People's Assemblies

  1. A Vichial People's Assembly may deliberate on matters affecting its vichy and its inhabitants.
  2. It may adopt recommendations, resolutions and opinions for submission to the Archvichy, Vichy Council or national institutions.
  3. It may scrutinise the administration of its vichy in accordance with procedures established by law.
  4. Its functions shall remain subordinate to the constitutional order and shall not include independent executive authority.

Article 112 — Relationship Between the Assembly and Vichy Council

  1. The Vichial People's Assembly and the Vichy Council shall remain separate institutions with distinct constitutional functions.
  2. The Assembly shall perform representative, deliberative and scrutiny functions.
  3. The Vichy Council shall perform executive and administrative functions under the direction of the Archvichy.
  4. Neither institution shall assume the functions constitutionally assigned to the other.

Article 113 — Sessions and Proceedings of Vichial People's Assemblies

  1. Each Vichial People's Assembly shall convene in accordance with procedures established by law or its internal rules.
  2. Its rules shall provide for the conduct of sessions, deliberations, voting and the keeping of records.
  3. Extraordinary sessions may be convened where required by the interests of the vichy or under procedures established by law.
  4. Proceedings shall remain within the constitutional functions of the Assembly.

Article 114 — Vichial Resolutions and Recommendations

  1. A Vichial People's Assembly may adopt resolutions and recommendations on matters within its remit.
  2. Such decisions may be submitted to the Archvichy, Vichy Council or relevant national institution.
  3. They shall be advisory unless given a specific legal effect under national law consistent with this Covenant.
  4. No vichial resolution shall override national legislation, executive orders or lawful directions of the Liquin.

Article 115 — Vichial Administration

  1. Vichial administration shall be conducted by the Archvichy and Vichy Council within the authority assigned to them.
  2. Vichial authorities shall implement national policy and applicable law within their territorial responsibilities.
  3. Administrative arrangements shall promote continuity, efficiency and accountability.
  4. Vichial authorities shall not establish institutions or offices contrary to this Covenant or national law.

Article 116 — Relationship with National Government

  1. Vichial institutions shall operate as subordinate components of the national constitutional and administrative order.
  2. The national government may issue lawful directions concerning the implementation of national policy and legislation.
  3. Vichial authorities shall provide information and reports to national institutions when lawfully required.
  4. Disagreements concerning the administration of national law shall be referred to the competent national authority for determination.

Article 117 — National Law and Vichial Compliance

  1. National legislation and executive orders lawfully issued under this Covenant shall apply throughout all vichies.
  2. Vichial institutions shall ensure that their acts and decisions conform to national law.
  3. No vichy or vichial institution may suspend, disregard or invalidate national law.
  4. Any vichial measure inconsistent with this Covenant or national law shall lack legal authority to the extent of the inconsistency.

Article 118 — Limits on Vichial Authority

  1. Vichial institutions shall not exercise powers reserved to the Liquin or national institutions.
  2. No vichy shall conduct independent foreign relations, establish a separate sovereign authority or claim a right of secession.
  3. No vichial institution shall enact measures contrary to the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  4. All delegated or assigned vichial authority shall remain subject to national supervision and the constitutional order.

Article 119 — Absence and Continuity of Vichial Office

  1. Each vichy shall maintain continuity of administration during vacancies, absences or incapacity affecting its officeholders.
  2. Temporary arrangements shall be made in accordance with procedures established by law or formal regulation.
  3. No temporary officeholder shall acquire permanent entitlement to an office solely by performing its duties during a vacancy.
  4. Vichial continuity arrangements shall not interrupt the authority of the national government.

Article 120 — Integrity of Vichial Government

  1. All vichial institutions and officeholders shall uphold the Supreme Liquindian Covenant and the unity of the Republic.
  2. The organisation and conduct of vichial government shall respect the distinct functions of the Archvichy, Vichy Council and Vichial People's Assembly.
  3. The national authority may regulate, supervise and, where constitutionally authorised, reorganise vichial arrangements in accordance with this Covenant.
  4. Nothing in this Chapter shall diminish the sovereignty, territorial integrity or indivisible constitutional character of the Republic of Liquindo.

Chapter VII — Rights and National Identity

Article 121 — The Liquindian National Community

  1. The Liquindian national community shall comprise all persons holding citizenship of the Republic of Liquindo.
  2. Citizenship shall constitute the legal basis of membership in the Liquindian nation.
  3. National identity shall be civic and cultural in character and shall not be determined by race, ancestry or ethnic origin.
  4. The state shall promote a shared sense of national belonging while respecting the varied backgrounds of citizens.

Article 122 — Citizenship of the Republic

  1. Citizenship of the Republic of Liquindo shall confer the legal status of a Liquindian citizen.
  2. No person shall be legally recognised as Liquindian without holding citizenship of the Republic.
  3. Every citizen shall possess the rights and bear the duties established by this Covenant and national law.
  4. Website membership, registration or participation in any non-state platform shall not confer or determine citizenship.

Article 123 — Acquisition of Citizenship

  1. Citizenship may be acquired by birth, descent, naturalisation or any other lawful means established by national legislation.
  2. Legislation shall prescribe the requirements, procedures and competent authorities for the grant or recognition of citizenship.
  3. Applications for citizenship shall be determined in accordance with law and the national interests of the Republic.
  4. No person shall acquire citizenship solely through website membership or an informal declaration of national identity.

Article 124 — Loss and Renunciation of Citizenship

  1. Citizenship may be relinquished or withdrawn only in circumstances and through procedures established by national law.
  2. No withdrawal of citizenship shall occur except under a lawful procedure that records the grounds for the decision.
  3. The law shall provide for the determination of questions concerning the status and continuity of citizenship.
  4. The exercise of powers under this Article shall remain subject to the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.

Article 125 — Equality of Citizens

  1. All citizens shall possess equal legal status as members of the Liquindian nation.
  2. No citizen shall be denied the protection of the law solely on grounds of race, ancestry, ethnic origin or place of birth.
  3. Public institutions shall administer the law consistently and shall not establish racial or ancestral distinctions in citizenship.
  4. Equality under this Article shall operate within the constitutional order and shall not prevent lawful distinctions based on relevant qualifications or responsibilities.

Article 126 — Dignity and Personal Security

  1. The state shall recognise the dignity and personal security of every person within its jurisdiction.
  2. Public authorities shall exercise their powers in accordance with law and shall not impose arbitrary punishment or detention.
  3. Any deprivation of liberty shall have a lawful basis and shall be carried out through procedures established by law.
  4. Legislation shall provide appropriate procedures for the review of measures affecting personal liberty.

Article 127 — Protection of the Person

  1. Every person shall be entitled to the protection of the law against violence, coercion and unlawful interference with their person.
  2. No public authority shall impose punishment except in accordance with law.
  3. Legal procedures concerning allegations of wrongdoing shall provide notice of the allegation and a meaningful opportunity to respond.
  4. The detailed safeguards and procedures governing criminal and civil matters shall be established by national law.

Article 128 — The Liquindian Language

  1. Liquindian shall be the sole official language of the Republic, as established by this Covenant.
  2. National institutions shall promote the use and development of Liquindian in public administration and national life.
  3. The state shall support the preservation, teaching and continued development of the language.
  4. The use of other languages in private, personal and cultural contexts shall remain permitted.

Article 129 — Language Learning and Proficiency

  1. The state shall encourage citizens and residents to learn and use the Liquindian language.
  2. Language proficiency may be required for defined public offices, official responsibilities or procedures where such proficiency is reasonably necessary.
  3. Any language requirement imposed by law shall be stated clearly and applied according to its specified purpose.
  4. Language learning and integration measures shall not be used to impose racial or ancestral conditions upon citizenship.

Article 130 — Cultural Identity and Heritage

  1. The state shall preserve and develop the cultural heritage, traditions and historical memory of Liquindo.
  2. Citizens shall be encouraged to participate in the cultural life of the Republic.
  3. The state may support cultural institutions, education and activities that contribute to the development of a shared national culture.
  4. Such measures shall respect the civic character of the Liquindian nation and shall not establish racial tests of belonging.

Article 131 — Cultural Integration

  1. The state shall encourage cultural integration and participation in the common life of the Liquindian nation.
  2. Integration shall be pursued through education, civic participation, language learning and cultural exchange.
  3. No person shall be compelled to abandon their personal background, ancestry or private cultural practices as a condition of belonging to the Republic.
  4. Integration policy shall seek common civic understanding while respecting the lawful freedom of individuals.

Article 132 — Freedom of Expression

  1. Every person shall be entitled to express opinions and communicate information, subject to the limitations established by law.
  2. Restrictions upon expression shall have a lawful basis and shall be consistent with the protection of national security, public order, the rights of others and the constitutional order.
  3. No restriction shall be imposed except by a competent authority acting within its lawful powers.
  4. The exercise of this freedom shall not confer authority to compel any state institution to adopt a particular opinion.

Article 133 — Association and Civic Participation

  1. Citizens shall be entitled to participate in lawful civic, cultural, social and political organisations.
  2. The establishment and operation of organisations shall be governed by national law and the provisions of this Covenant.
  3. Political organisation within any unified national political movement shall remain subject to the constitutional rules governing that movement.
  4. No association shall possess public authority unless such authority is conferred upon it by this Covenant or national law.

Article 134 — Public Participation

  1. Citizens shall be encouraged to contribute to the public life and development of the Republic.
  2. Such participation may take place through the National People's Council, Vichial People's Assemblies, lawful organisations, public consultation and other recognised channels.
  3. Public institutions may establish procedures for receiving petitions, proposals and representations from citizens.
  4. Participation under this Article shall not alter the distribution of constitutional authority established by this Covenant.

Article 135 — Access to Public Institutions

  1. Citizens shall have access to public institutions in accordance with the law and the procedures governing those institutions.
  2. Public authorities shall make available information concerning their functions and applicable public procedures, subject to lawful restrictions.
  3. Requests and applications submitted to public authorities shall be handled by the competent institution in accordance with applicable rules.
  4. Nothing in this Article shall require the disclosure of information lawfully protected for reasons of security, privacy or the proper administration of the state.

Article 136 — Duties of Citizens

  1. Every citizen shall owe loyalty to the Republic of Liquindo and respect its sovereignty and constitutional order.
  2. Citizens shall observe the Supreme Liquindian Covenant and laws lawfully enacted under it.
  3. Citizens shall respect the rights of others and contribute, according to their abilities and lawful obligations, to the welfare and development of the national community.
  4. Specific civic duties may be prescribed by national legislation, provided that such legislation conforms to this Covenant.

Article 137 — Public Service and Responsibility

  1. Citizens may serve the Republic through public office, public service and other lawful forms of national contribution.
  2. Public office shall be exercised in the interests of the Republic and in accordance with the duties attached to the office.
  3. Eligibility requirements for public service may include qualifications, experience, language proficiency and other criteria established by law.
  4. No eligibility requirement shall be based solely upon race or ancestry.

Article 138 — Rights of Non-Citizens

  1. Persons who are not citizens shall remain subject to the laws of the Republic while within its jurisdiction.
  2. Non-citizens shall receive the protection of the law in matters concerning personal security and lawful treatment.
  3. Rights and privileges reserved by this Covenant or national legislation to citizens shall not automatically extend to non-citizens.
  4. The conditions governing residence, entry, departure and any route to citizenship shall be established by national law.

Article 139 — Limits and Protection of Rights

  1. The rights recognised in this Chapter shall be exercised consistently with the sovereignty, security and constitutional order of the Republic and the rights of others.
  2. Any limitation upon a right shall require a basis in this Covenant or national law and shall be imposed by a competent authority.
  3. No limitation shall be interpreted as authorising the abolition of the civic character of the Liquindian nation or the supremacy of this Covenant.
  4. All public institutions shall respect the rights and protections established by this Chapter when exercising their powers.

Article 140 — Interpretation of Rights and National Identity

  1. This Chapter shall be interpreted in a manner consistent with the foundational principles of the Supreme Liquindian Covenant.
  2. The rights and duties established herein shall be read together with the sovereignty of the Republic, National Rupturalism and the civic and cultural character of the Liquindian nation.
  3. Nothing in this Chapter shall create a separate legal definition of Liquindian identity apart from citizenship as established by this Covenant.
  4. Further procedures and protections may be established by national legislation, provided that they remain consistent with this Covenant.

Chapter VIII — Economic Organisation

Liquindo supports public ownership of major industries and an economy organised in accordance with National Rupturalism. The detailed constitutional distribution of ownership, economic administration and public responsibilities remains to be consolidated.

Chapter IX — Constitutional Procedures

This chapter governs constitutional interpretation, institutional review, disputes and emergency procedures. The record identifies agreed revisions to Articles 163–167, but their complete final wording must be restored before this chapter can be treated as complete.
