Liquindo, officially the Federal Republic of Liquindo, is envisioned as a crowdfunded island nation; a refuge from global instability, an experimental society blending modern innovation, traditional values, and self-sufficiency.

Etymology

Liquindo is a play on the Portuguese words "Lindo" and "Liquido" translating to "Beautiful" and "Liquid", the latter referring to Water.

History

Liquindo's primary conception was thought of in 2021 when Diego Liquin was still in secondary school. The idea was inspired by the Netflix documentary film Rose Island.

Since 2021, there have been multiple revivals of Liquindo, most notably from Finn Vaughton (2023) and Edward Fullwood (2025). The latter which is ongoing. The first physical Liquindo flag ever created was made on the 3rd of July 2025, and other merchandise has been created such as pin-badges and stickers.

Development of the Liquindian language started on the 16th of November 2025. It currently has 200+ words.

Vexillology

Liquindo has undergone three flag changes since its establishment in 2021 but maintains its current flag: a yellow, navy, and white tricolor with a yin-yang charge rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise, recolored to navy and yellow. It also hosts a star in the upper-middle of the yin-yang. The star signifies authority, the navy being integrity, mustard being the people and the white being faith.

Government

As Liquindo's development has not started, such as buying the island, the following information is theoretical.

Liquindo would operate as a junta, led by Diego Liquin and below him his investors who would sway and appeal decisions.

Pre-Democratization

In a time pre-democratization, Liquindo would operate as an elective autocracy. The first dictator (Diego Liquin) would appoint 5 political advisors. After his death, these advisors would elect a new dictator. The new dictator would then appoint 5 new advisors, allowing the cycle to repeat itself. The dictator's powers would be bound by his advisors, and since he appointed them himself, there would be no political struggle unless the advisors truly doubt the dictator's ability to rule.

Permanent Structure

Liquindo would be an autocratic regime ruled under the administration of Liquin's Plan. The director would serve as head of state. Instead of democracy, parties would be assimilated into the administration, under the approval of Liquin and those already accepted, and converted into factions, which the public can join. This would avoid any severe political interference in contrast to the administration's ideologies.

Political Factions

Factions are represented by the populace, and hence Liquindo is a one-party state. Political parties are replaced by factions. However, only selected factions are accepted if they align with Liquindo's current ideology. So factions can be rejected more often than parties in other nations.
