Liquindo, officially the Federal Republic of Liquindo, is envisioned as a crowdfunded island nation and experimental society emphasizing self-sufficiency, traditional values, and modern development.

Etymology

The name Liquindo is a play on the Portuguese words lindo and líquido, meaning “beautiful” and “liquid” respectively. The latter refers to water.

History

Liquindo's initial conception dates to 2021, when Diego Liquin was still in secondary school. The idea was inspired by the film Rose Island .

Since 2021, the project has undergone several revivals, most notably involving Finn Vaughton in 2023 and Edward Fullwood in 2025.

The first physical Liquindo flag was produced on 3 July 2025. Other merchandise, including badges and stickers, was later produced.

Development of the Liquindian language began on 16 November 2025 and has since produced more than 200 words.

Vexillology

Liquindo has used several flag designs since its establishment. The current design consists of yellow, navy, and white, with a rotated yin-yang emblem and a star positioned within the central design.

Within Liquindian symbolism, the star represents authority, navy represents integrity, mustard yellow represents the people, and white represents faith.

Government

The following describes proposed institutions. Liquindo has not acquired sovereign territory and these structures are theoretical.

Liquindo is envisioned as a highly centralized political system headed by a director, with advisers and affiliated political factions participating within the wider administration.

Early proposed structure

An earlier proposal described an elective autocratic structure in which the head of government would appoint a council of political advisers. Following the end of a director's tenure, that council would participate in choosing a successor.

Permanent structure

Later proposals describe Liquindo as being administered through Liquin's Plan, with the director serving as head of state. Political organizations would operate as factions within the administrative system rather than as conventional competing political parties.

Defence

Liquindo's theoretical defence doctrine emphasizes national resilience, civil defence, emergency preparedness, territorial protection, and deterrence rather than foreign expansion.

The concept also assigns defence institutions civilian roles during disasters and infrastructure emergencies, including logistics, reconstruction, emergency response, and continuity of essential services.

Proposed military culture emphasizes discipline, public service, institutional responsibility, and defensive readiness.

Economy

Liquindo's proposed economy is based on the principle that economic activity should serve national self-sufficiency, families, social stability, and long-term development.

Strategic industries such as infrastructure, banking, energy, and heavy industry are envisioned as being heavily directed or owned by the state.

Agriculture is envisioned as more decentralized, with family-owned farms forming an important part of rural economic life.

The system places less emphasis on unrestricted market competition and speculative finance, instead proposing substantial state coordination of production and strategic resources.

Domestic production and self-sufficiency are major goals, although limited trade and private economic activity may continue where compatible with wider national objectives.

Overall, the proposed system combines centralized strategic planning with smaller-scale private and family economic activity.

Political Factions

Liquindo's proposed political system replaces conventional competing political parties with internal factions operating within the wider administrative structure.

Factions may represent different political tendencies while remaining subject to the institutional rules of the administration.
