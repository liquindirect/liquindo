The Liquindian Integralist Front, commonly referred to just as the Liquindian Integralists, is a political faction in Liquindo. The faction was founded under the objective of shaping Liquindo's national identity through increased Catholic influence, by uniting Church and state.

History

The Liquindian Integralist Front was founded around the same time as the National Development Faction, in late December of 2025. It was created as there was a rising influx of Catholic Christian support of the LNF, however the LNF did not share the same sentiments about secularism as they did.

Currents

There are two currents in this faction: an integralist current, which forms the mainstream of the faction, in addition to a theocratic current.
