About & settings

App information, privacy and account-session guidance.

Liquindo Android

This app uses the official Liquindo website and the same Supabase account system. It does not create a separate account.

Login sessions are managed by the website's authentication system. Closing the app does not intentionally sign you out. Use Log out on the website when you want to end a session.

For privacy information, visit the Liquindo privacy notice. APK updates are downloaded from GitHub and Android asks you to confirm installation.
