Flag Symbolism

The second variant of the latest Vaughtonology flag contains the slogan "Soek, Doodmaak, Beheer", which translates into English as "Seek, Kill, Control". Within Vaughtonology, the slogan is presented as a reference to the ideology's aggressive stance on immigration.

The flag also draws on religious symbolism. Psalm 33:12 states: "Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, the people he chose for his inheritance." Within Vaughtonology, this verse is used to express the movement's strong Protestant influence and its belief that national prosperity is connected to religious devotion.

This religious emphasis reflects the extent to which Vaughtonology has historically associated national identity with Protestant Christianity, at times approaching a theocratic conception of the state.

An earlier version of the flag included an explicitly anti-foreigner slogan rendered in multiple languages, including Korean and Afrikaans. The slogan reflected a more provocative stage in the ideology's development.

The inclusion of Korean was intentionally ironic, as later Vaughtonology developed anti-Korean positions. This has been described as a remnant of the movement's origins as a joke ideology. As Vaughtonology became more developed and internally consistent, the Korean wording was removed from the later flag variant.
