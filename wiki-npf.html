The Nuclear Proliferation Faction, commonly abbreviated as the NPF, is a political faction in Liquindo. The faction advocates nuclear proliferation and opposes the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons. Its doctrine argues that nuclear weapons provide states with international status and deterrence against substantially larger powers.

History

The NPF was founded in late December 2025, along with the National Development Faction. It was partly inspired by the now-defunct Party of the Reconstruction of the National Order (P.R.O.N.A), a Brazilian nationalist party which advocated nuclear-weapons development as part of its political program.

Currents

There are two currents inside the NPF: the standard nationalist current and the fringe accelerationist current.

Standard Current

The Standard Current believes that weapons of mass destruction are necessary for gaining international status and providing strategic deterrence. Within its doctrine, nuclear deterrence is viewed as a complement to Liquindo's conventional defensive forces, particularly against states possessing substantially greater military power.

Accelerationist Current

The accelerationist current advocates what it describes as a global palingenesis: the belief that the existing world order must collapse or be radically transformed before a new period of peace and prosperity can emerge.
