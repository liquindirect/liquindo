Political faction of Liquindo

The Nuclear Proliferation Faction, commonly abbreviated as the NPF, is a political faction associated with Liquindo.

Its political doctrine places particular emphasis on nuclear deterrence, national sovereignty, and the role of nuclear weapons in international relations.

The faction has expressed opposition to the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons and argues that nuclear capability can increase the strategic influence of smaller states.

This article describes positions attributed to the NPF. Nuclear proliferation and threats involving weapons of mass destruction carry severe humanitarian, environmental, and international security risks.

History

The NPF was founded in late December 2025 alongside the National Development Faction.

Its development was partly influenced by historical nationalist political movements that supported independent national nuclear programs.

One cited influence was Brazil's former Party of the Reconstruction of the National Order (PRONA), whose political program included support for a Brazilian nuclear-weapons capability.

Political Doctrine

The NPF views nuclear strategy primarily through the concepts of deterrence, sovereignty, strategic independence, and international power.

Within this framework, supporters argue that possession of a nuclear deterrent can reduce the vulnerability of a small state to coercion by significantly stronger powers.

Critics of this position argue that wider nuclear proliferation increases the probability of miscalculation, escalation, proliferation to additional actors, and catastrophic humanitarian consequences.

Currents

Two broad tendencies have been associated with the NPF: a standard nationalist current and a smaller accelerationist current.

Standard Current

The standard current treats nuclear weapons primarily as an instrument of strategic deterrence.

Its supporters argue that a smaller state may seek a powerful deterrent when conventional military forces would be insufficient against a substantially larger adversary.

This current generally presents deterrence as defensive rather than as a justification for initiating conflict.

Accelerationist Current

A fringe accelerationist tendency has also been associated with the faction.

It has been described as believing that the existing international order must collapse or undergo extreme disruption before a new political order can emerge.

Unlike the standard current, this tendency has historically treated escalating instability and political violence as desirable.

Such ideas are highly controversial because deliberately promoting large-scale conflict or political violence would endanger civilians and undermine international security.

Nuclear Proliferation

Nuclear proliferation refers to the spread of nuclear weapons, weapons-related technology, or weapons programs to additional states or actors.

International efforts to limit proliferation have developed partly from concern that an increasing number of nuclear-armed states raises the risks of nuclear conflict, accidents, miscalculation, and arms races.

The NPF disputes some of these arguments and instead emphasizes the deterrent effect associated with nuclear arsenals.

Relationship with Liquin's Plan

The NPF operates as an internal political faction within Liquin's Plan rather than as an independent political party.

Its primary role within the factional system is to represent a political tendency focused on nuclear policy, national security, strategic independence, and deterrence.
